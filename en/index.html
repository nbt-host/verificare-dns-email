DNS and email check.

Enter a domain and see right away where the website points, who receives the email and whether SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set up correctly, explained in plain English. Free, no account.

What it checks

  • A and AAAA — the address of the server the website is on.
  • NS — the nameservers, that is, where the domain's DNS is managed: at NBT Host, at Cloudflare or at the company you bought the domain from.
  • MX — the servers that receive email for the addresses on the domain.
  • SPF — the list of servers allowed to send email on behalf of the domain.
  • DKIM — the public key the recipient uses to verify your messages' signature. It sits under a “selector”: on NBT Host (Enhance) hosting the selector is hostingcp; Google Workspace usually uses google, and Microsoft 365 uses selector1 and selector2. We try all of them automatically, plus the one you enter.
  • DMARC — your rule for messages that claim to come from the domain but fail SPF or DKIM.
  • CAA — which authorities may issue SSL certificates for the domain.

Open source tool, MIT licence, maintained by NBT Host — European web hosting, VPS and domains, on servers in the EU. Source code: github.com/nbt-host/verificare-dns-email. Everything runs in your browser; we do not store or send the domains you check anywhere.
